# SESTRAV v2.0 Outreach and Networking Strategy

This document defines the post-release outreach, networking, and partnership strategies for the SESTRAV project, ensuring clean isolation from the core technical and architectural roadmap.

## 1. Stakeholder & External Peer Evaluation

As part of the post-release validation and feedback loop, the following outreach activities are planned:

| Activity | Goal | Owner |
|:---|:---|:---|
| External peer evaluation | Engage 2 to 3 computational immunology groups to test installation, Snakemake ergonomics, and feature extraction | Gavin Borges |

## 2. Wet-Lab Partnership & Collaboration

To validate the model's predictive power on real biological samples, we will seek experimental partnerships:
- **Boston Biotech Network**: Leverage the Boston biotech network to identify and secure a partner lab for assay execution (contingent on partnership).

## 3. Collaboration & Visibility Success Metrics

These metrics track the success of our external outreach, branding, and collaboration efforts:

| Metric | Target |
|:---|:---|
| External citation or fork | At least one within 90 days of release |
| Wet-lab collaboration MOU | Signed by Month 6 (dependent on partner availability) |
