## How to be listed here

New contributors are welcome and credited. See `CONTRIBUTING.md` for the
contribution workflow and for examples of **significant** contributions the
project is actively seeking (new pathogen/allele adapters, external-dataset
validation, documentation, and independent review). Significant, sustained
contributors may be invited to become maintainers per `GOVERNANCE.md`.
